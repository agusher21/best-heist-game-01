# The Silent Ledger

A top-down stealth heist game set in the Meridian Bank at 02:00. Play as Wren, a master thief infiltrating the vault hall to steal the golden Meridian Ledger. Evade three patrolling guards with dynamic vision cones, dodge two sweeping security cameras, and use your wits — throw coins to distract guards, sneak to halve detection, and cut the power once per run to blind every camera and shrink guard vision for 10 seconds. Grab the Ledger, unlock the exit, and ghost out clean.

## How to Play

- **Move:** WASD or Arrow keys
- **Sneak:** Hold Shift (half speed, half detection rate)
- **Throw Coin:** Click mouse (3 coins; guards investigate the landing spot)
- **Interact:** Press E (grab loot, open exit once Ledger is held)
- **Cut Power:** Press Q (once per run, 10 seconds — cameras go blind, guard cones shrink, alarm freezes)
- **Pause:** P or Esc
- **Start / Restart:** Space or Click

Steal the **Meridian Ledger** (required, 500 pts) and up to **3 Gold Bars** (100 pts each, optional). Each carried item slows you by 8%. The **Alarm Meter** rises when seen (guards ~25/s, cameras ~40/s) and decays when hidden (~8/s). At 100: **LOCKDOWN** — all guards converge for 10 seconds. Survive and the alarm drops to 60.

**Win:** Grab the Ledger, reach the green EXIT. Score = loot + time bonus − alarm peaks.
